A little about me

Where science, research and real-world decisions meet

My academic background is in Microbiology and Public Health. These disciplines have shaped my interest in understanding health challenges, examining evidence and finding practical ways to improve decisions.

My research experience includes work on non-communicable disease prevention, healthcare financing and primary healthcare, as well as work-related stress and mental wellbeing among Community Health Extension Workers.

Alongside research, I have experience in procurement, logistics, inventory and relationship management. These roles have given me a practical understanding of operations, reporting, coordination and the importance of accurate information.

I am interested in using data analysis and health information systems to make research more useful, improve reporting and support better decisions. I value work that is thoughtful, evidence-based and relevant to people's lives.
